Chapter 01 · Predictions & instant decisions

Will

will / won’t+infinitive

Predictions, opinions, decisions made now, offers and promises.

ESlloverá · ya contesto yo · te lo llevo

Prediction · opinion
I think it will rain tomorrow.
Decision made now
The phone’s ringing! — I’ll answer it.
Offer · promise
I’ll carry it for you.
I promise I won’t tell anyone.
Careful!
I will to go · She will goes
She will go — no to, no -s.
Chapter 02 · Plans & evidence

Be going to

am / is / are+going to+infinitive

Plans decided before speaking — and predictions with evidence.

ESvoy a estudiar medicina · va a llover

Plan · intention
I’m going to study medicine at university.
Evidence you can see
Look at those black clouds! It’s going to rain.
Negative
She isn’t going to come.
Question
Are you going to watch the match?
Chapter 03 · Which one?

Will or going to?

The key question: when did you decide?
WILL
GOING TO
Decided now
Oh no, there's no milk! I'll buy some.
Decided before
I'm going to buy milk on my way home.
Prediction: opinion
I think Spain will win.
Prediction: evidence
They're 3–0 up! They're going to win!
Chapter 04 · Arrangements & timetables

Present tenses

Arrangements → present continuous.
Timetables → present simple.

ESquedo con Laura a las seis · el tren sale a las 8:15

FRIDAY
6 pm meet Laura
9 am fly to London
DEPARTURES
08:15TRAINON TIME
21:00FILMSTARTS
Arrangement · present continuous
I’m meeting Laura at six tomorrow.
It’s in your diary
We’re flying to London on Friday.
Timetable · present simple
The train leaves at 8:15.
The film starts at nine.
Arrangements → I'm meeting · Timetables → it leaves
Chapter 05 · In progress in the future

Future continuous

will be+verb-ing

An action that will be in progress at a moment in the future.

ESmañana a esta hora estaré tumbado en la playa

At that moment
This time tomorrow, I’ll be lying on the beach.
At that moment
At 9 pm tonight, we’ll be having dinner.
Polite question about plans
Will you be using the car this evening?
Chapter 06 · Finished before…

Future perfect

will have+past participle

An action that will be finished before a moment in the future.

ESpara junio habré terminado los exámenes

by Fridayby next yearby the time you arrive
Finished by then
By the end of June, I’ll have finished all my exams.
Finished by then
By 2050, scientists will have found a cure.
Negative
Sorry, I won’t have finished by then.
Chapter 07 · when · as soon as · until · if

When + present

whenas soon asuntilbeforeafterif

After these words we use a present tense — never will — even for the future.

ESte llamaré cuando llegue (¡no “cuando llegaré”!)

WHEN / IF …
+ present
will
,
MAIN CLAUSE
will + verb
Classic mistake
I’ll call you when I will arrive.
I’ll call you when I arrive.
as soon as
As soon as she gets home, she’ll text you.
if
If it rains, we’ll stay at home.
until
We’ll wait here until the bus comes.
